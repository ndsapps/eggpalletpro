Privacy policy

EggPalletPro is an egg count and pallet weight calculator. This policy describes what the app does with information.

Information stored on your device

The app stores egg counts and pallet weight information only on your device. That information is not sent to the developer. Uninstalling the app removes it from the device.

Information the developer does not collect

EggPalletPro does not include analytics, advertising, or crash reporting that sends data to the developer. The developer does not receive your egg counts, pallet weights, location, contacts, or photos.

Purchases

If you subscribe, payment is processed by Apple through In-App Purchase. The developer does not receive your card number. Apple provides the developer with subscription status needed to unlock features. Apple’s handling of payment data is covered by Apple’s privacy policy.

Children

EggPalletPro is not directed at children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
